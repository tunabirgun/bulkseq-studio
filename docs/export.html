Preserve the cross-study record#

Keep the full meta result, the eligibility ledger and the dedicated report. A convergent-gene export is a selected view, not a separate reanalysis. Retain per-study effects so readers can assess whether the pooled summary represents the studies.

FilePurpose
results/meta/meta_analysis_results.csvCombined statistics, pooled effects and per-study columns.
results/meta/meta_eligibility.jsonIncluded/excluded studies, retained genes, model formulas, contrast, testing families and losses.
checks/17_meta_analysis_qc.jsonStatus and checks for the meta-analysis branch.
results/reports/meta_analysis_report.htmlDedicated cross-study report when generated.
Additional meta-analysis exports

Depending on enabled steps, exports include meta_convergent_genes.csv, meta_study_summary.csv, comparative figures, per-study tables and cross-study enrichment. Export only files that exist for the completed route. Optional enrichment may be skipped when annotation or mapping requirements are not met.

The ledger records the source and included studies, replicate totals, per-study filtering, shared complete cases, pooled failures, both testing families and their sizes, and the executed factor, contrast and formulas. Preserve it with any selected table.
