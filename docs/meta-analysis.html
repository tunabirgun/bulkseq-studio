How-to guides

Combine independent studies

Compare the same contrast across studies without treating studies as extra samples.

On this page
  • 1. Add study membership
  • 2. Set the meta-analysis controls
  • 3. Understand what is combined
  • 4. Review the separate report

Before you start, establish that the studies are independent and address the same directionally defined comparison in one organism and compatible gene identifiers. Re-deposited samples are not an independent replication.

1. Add study membership#

Bring the samples into a project and fill dataset and condition on Samples. Each dataset identifies its study of origin, while condition identifies the compared groups.

Caution

A study enters the per-study combination only with at least two replicates in each compared arm, and the combination needs at least two such studies. With fewer it does not run, and check 17 says so; the joint analysis still runs.

Check

Confirm enough studies remain: excluded studies and the reason for each are listed in results/meta/excluded_studies.txt.

2. Set the meta-analysis controls#

Multi-study meta-analysis: offAdds separate study fits and combination alongside the joint analysis.Enable for independent studies of the same contrast; it is not a way to turn technical repeats into replication.

3. Understand what is combined#

In the published baseline, each eligible study is fitted separately with DESeq2 using the contrast factor only. The per-study fits do not inherit extra covariates from the joint design. P-values are combined with a replicate-weighted inverse-normal method; unshrunken effects are also combined with an effect-size model. The effect model is fixed-effect for two studies and DerSimonian–Laird random-effect for three or more.

Direction-discordant genes remain visible but are not called convergent meta-DEGs. The joint analysis adds dataset to any additive design that omits it, the default ~ condition or one you typed such as ~ batch + condition, provided every term is a sample-sheet column and the result stays full rank. A design with an interaction or nesting operator, or one where adding dataset would be rank-deficient, is left as written and the log says so. A combined p-value is not a guarantee of biological consistency or increased power.

Cross-study enrichment applies the configured per-study FDR and absolute log2-fold-change thresholds before restricting each up/down selection to the shared tested universe. The FDR boundary is strict, the effect boundary is inclusive, and an exact zero effect remains neutral when the effect threshold is zero. It then uses the main enrichment route's identifier policy for official identifiers, the configured keytype and aliases. Exactly one accepted Entrez mapping is used for both the shared universe and every study or convergent foreground. Unresolved one-to-many mappings are excluded rather than selected by database row order. Inspect results/meta/meta_enrichment_mapping.tsv for accepted, unmapped and ambiguous identifiers and check 18 for universe and foreground coverage. An ambiguity finding remains review-required when enrichment later skips for a small set or no terms.

4. Review the separate report#

Inspect meta_analysis_report.html, the tables under results/meta/, constituent-study results and meta-specific checks. Review excluded studies, discordant genes and duplicate-study warnings.

Warning

Do not present the joint model and the meta-analysis combination as the same result.

Check

Check that a claimed cross-study finding is supported by the applicable per-study direction and eligibility evidence.

Advanced: design limits

If the question needs within-study covariate adjustment beyond the documented contrast-only fits, this baseline does not implement that model through the meta switch. Review historical meta-analysis notices before reusing older outputs.

New in 0.32.0

Cross-study enrichment now uses the same FDR and absolute log2-fold-change thresholds as the published per-study up/down tables before applying the shared tested universe and accepted identifier mapping. Exact effect boundaries remain included; zero remains neutral when the threshold is zero. The convergent meta-DEG policy and pooling models are unchanged. Synthetic tests establish exact foreground membership, but no real meta-analysis or enrichment output has been rerun under 0.32.0.

New in 0.32.0

The separate cross-study report now uses the main report’s native figure viewer. Comparative figures open in a labelled modal with Fit, Actual size and zoom controls; Escape and Close return keyboard focus to the figure that opened it. This changes report viewing only: no meta-analysis figure, table or result is recalculated.
