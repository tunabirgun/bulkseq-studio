Analysis setup

Combine independent studies

Compare the same contrast across studies without treating studies as extra samples.

On this page
  • 1. Add study membership
  • 2. Set the meta-analysis controls
  • 3. Understand what is combined
  • 4. Review the separate report

Before you start, establish that the studies are independent and address the same directionally defined comparison in one organism and compatible gene identifiers. Re-deposited samples are not an independent replication.

1. Add study membership#

Bring the samples into a project and fill dataset and condition on Samples. Each dataset identifies its study of origin, while condition identifies the compared groups.

Check before proceeding

At least one study must contain both compared arms to avoid perfect study/group confounding. Studies without both arms are excluded from the per-study combination and reported; confirm enough eligible studies remain.

2. Set the meta-analysis controls#

SettingWhat it changesWhen to change it / example
Multi-study meta-analysis: offAdds separate study fits and combination alongside the joint analysis.Enable for independent studies of the same contrast; it is not a way to turn technical repeats into replication.
datasetSeparates the per-study fits and supports study adjustment in the joint model.Illustration: study_A and study_B each include treated and control samples.
Per-study enrichment: offAdds enrichment for each constituent study.Enable if those separate functional comparisons are required; it adds computation and service use.
Meta GO ontology: BPSelects biological process, molecular function (MF) or cellular component (CC).Choose the annotation aspect matching your question, not the one yielding the most terms.
Meta figure labels 10 / heatmap genes 50 / enrichment terms 6Limits displayed content in comparative figures.Changing display counts does not change which genes pass the meta-analysis criteria.

3. Understand what is combined#

In the published baseline, each eligible study is fitted separately with DESeq2 using the contrast factor only. The per-study fits do not inherit extra covariates from the joint design. P-values are combined with a replicate-weighted inverse-normal method; unshrunken effects are also combined with an effect-size model. The effect model is fixed-effect for two studies and DerSimonian–Laird random-effect for three or more.

Direction-discordant genes remain visible but are not called convergent meta-DEGs. The joint default ~ condition design adds dataset adjustment, while an explicitly supplied multi-term design must be reviewed for appropriate study adjustment. A combined p-value is not a guarantee of biological consistency or increased power.

4. Review the separate report#

Inspect meta_analysis_report.html, the tables under results/meta/, constituent-study results and meta-specific checks. Review excluded studies, discordant genes and duplicate-study warnings.

Check before proceeding

Do not present the joint model and the meta-analysis combination as the same result. Check that a claimed cross-study finding is supported by the applicable per-study direction and eligibility evidence.

Advanced: design and archive limits

If the question needs within-study covariate adjustment beyond the documented contrast-only fits, this baseline does not implement that model through the meta switch. The separately versioned validation archive does not establish universal meta-analysis performance. Review historical meta-analysis notices before reusing older outputs.
