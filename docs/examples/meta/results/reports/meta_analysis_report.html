How to read the result fields

The display-derived per-study up/down summary also requires the figure-setting absolute log2 fold change ≥ not applied in this synthetic fixture; a non-significant study does not establish no effect.

combined_padj
Combined-p FDR from the inverse-normal p-value test, adjusted among matching-sign genes; a small value alone does not prove replication.
meta_sig
Combined-p FDR below the run threshold (0.05) with matching nonzero per-study effect signs; individual studies need not reach FDR.
common_direction
Up or down means all retained effects have that sign; discordant includes opposite-sign and zero-effect rows.
n_studies_sig
Number of included studies with per-study adjusted p-value below the run threshold (0.05); this count does not apply the fold-change threshold.
rem_log2FC; rem_ci_lo/hi
Pooled effect and its 95% confidence interval from the common-effect fit at two studies or DerSimonian–Laird random-effects fit at three or more.
rem_padj
BH adjusted across every estimable pooled-effect test, including opposite-sign and neutral rows.
tau2; I2; QEp
Between-study heterogeneity fields are deliberately omitted for the two-study common-effect fit; omission is a workflow reporting policy.
