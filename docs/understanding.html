What the volcano controls change#

Log2 fold change describes the size and direction of a difference. A value of 1 corresponds to a two-fold increase in the recorded comparison; −1 corresponds to one-half. The adjusted p-value summarizes statistical evidence after multiple-testing adjustment.

The homepage volcano uses fixed simulated results. Changing either cutoff changes which points are selected. It does not fit a new model or move the genes. A post hoc fold-change screen is not the same as testing an effect-size threshold.

An adjusted p-value is not the probability that one gene is a false finding. The interpretation of a rejection family does not automatically carry over to arbitrary subsets selected afterward.

Read agreement across studies carefully#

A combined meta hit has matching nonzero effect directions and passes the combined adjusted-p criterion. That does not mean every study was individually significant, that the studies are independent replications, or that the pooled effect applies to every setting.

When the combined and pooled results differ

The combined test combines per-study p-values after restricting the adjusted family to matching directions. The pooled test uses unshrunken effect sizes and their standard errors, with adjustment across every estimable pooled test. Different hypotheses, weights and testing families can produce different conclusions. Inspect the per-study effects and uncertainty rather than choosing whichever summary passes a threshold.

For two studies, a common-effect summary assumes a shared underlying effect; the software’s omission of heterogeneity statistics does not validate that assumption. With three or more studies, a random-effects fit still cannot repair confounding, incompatible contrasts or dependent studies. The workflow uses z-based Wald inference for pooled tests and intervals, which can be overconfident with few studies; assess those results as exploratory.

Questions to answer before interpretation
  • Do the included studies address a compatible contrast with aligned direction?
  • Are the biological samples independent across studies?
  • Which studies or genes were excluded, and why?
  • Are the joint-model and per-study formulas appropriate for the study designs?
  • Does the pooled estimate conceal important variation between studies?
  • Were older pooled-effect adjusted values recomputed under v0.34.0 or later?

A nonsignificant study is not proof of no effect. A matching-sign result is not a causal claim. Review the design, uncertainty and recorded exclusions before interpreting the biology.
